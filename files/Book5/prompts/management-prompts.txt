CHAPTER 5.2 PROMPTS: WORKFLOWS FOR MANAGERS AND PROJECT LEADERS
Fictional sample file for Microsoft Copilot Visual Bible, Book 5, Chapter 5.2. Text copied from the online chapter.

HOW TO USE THIS FILE
Copy only the text between the two long lines of dashes. Do not copy the headings, the dashes, or the notes. Replace anything in [square brackets] with your own details, or with the suggested values in the notes at the end.

----------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT 1  (section: Workflow 1: Prepare a decision)
----------------------------------------------------------------
I need to decide [the decision] by [date]. Using what's been written about this in email, chats, meetings, and files I can access, prepare a one-page brief with:
1. The decision, in one sentence.
2. Two or three options, with the main benefit, cost, and risk of each, and the evidence for each point.
3. What each person involved has said, with a source.
4. What we don't know, and who could tell us.
Don't recommend an option. Mark anything you're unsure about with [CHECK].
----------------------------------------------------------------

----------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT 2  (section: Workflow 1: Prepare a decision)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Which option is best supported by the evidence, and what would have to be true for each of the others to be the better choice?
----------------------------------------------------------------

----------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT 3  (section: Workflow 3: Track risks, dependencies, and commitments)
----------------------------------------------------------------
From meeting recaps, the project channel, and email about [project] in the last two weeks, make three lists:
1. Commitments: who promised what to whom, and by when. Mark any that are past their date with no update.
2. Dependencies: work that's waiting on something from another person or team.
3. Risks: concerns that anyone has raised, whether or not anyone responded.
Quote the source for each item. Include commitments that I made.
----------------------------------------------------------------

----------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT 4  (What to ask next)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Does this brief give more space to some people's views than others? Whose position is supported by the least evidence, and who hasn't been heard from at all?
----------------------------------------------------------------

----------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT 5  (section: Workflow 2: Produce a plan or a status report)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Compare with the attached previous report. List what changed, and anything that was promised last time and isn't mentioned this time.
----------------------------------------------------------------

----------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT 6  (From Chapter 3.9: status report prompt (the chapter says to reuse it; change "the rollout project" to your project))
----------------------------------------------------------------
Draft a status report for the rollout project for the week ending [date]. Use the action list, the project channel, and email about the project from the last seven days. For each milestone, give a status of on track, at risk, or late, with the evidence for it and a link to the source. List actions completed this week, actions due next week, and anything waiting on a decision. If you can't find evidence for a status, write [NO EVIDENCE] instead of guessing.
----------------------------------------------------------------
